[h1]Clothed Vampires[/h1]

[i]Dress your vampires and let them walk in the sun.[/i]

In the normal game, a sanguophage in sunlight is slowed down and upset by it, no matter what they're wearing, and with Alpha Genes a vampire can be knocked out or burned alive by the sun even when wrapped from head to toe. Clothed Vampires fixes that: if a sunlight-sensitive pawn's [b]torso and legs are covered by clothing[/b], the sun stops hurting them.

[b]Want to know exactly what each setting does?[/b]
[url=https://github.com/DaraynLB/ClothedVampires/blob/main/docs/settings-guide.md]The player's guide to the settings[/url] explains every option in plain words, with examples.

[b]Found a bug, or have a request?[/b]
Please log it on the [url=https://github.com/DaraynLB/ClothedVampires/issues]GitHub Issues page[/url] rather than the comments below. It's much easier for me to track and follow up on there.

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] RimWorld 1.6 or 1.7, with Biotech. Both versions are supported, and the game loads the right one automatically.
[*] Harmony: required, load before Clothed Vampires
[*] Optional: Vanilla Expanded Framework (comes with Alpha Genes) for the knockout and burn protection
[/list]

[h2]What it does[/h2]
A pawn counts as [b]covered[/b] when clothing covers both the torso and the legs:
[list]
[*] One item that covers both is enough, such as a duster.
[*] So is a shirt plus pants.
[*] A shirt on its own isn't enough. The legs are still in the sun.
[*] [b]Underwear doesn't count.[/b] Underwear from UnderWhere or Undergarments Core leaves your pawn exposed, even a one-piece set. Put real clothes on over it and they're protected.
[/list]

While covered and standing in sunlight, a pawn is spared:
[list]
[*] [b]Slowness[/b] from sunlight genes. A colonist with mild UV sensitivity walks at full speed instead of 90%.
[*] [b]Bad mood[/b] from the sun, such as "Sunlight sensitivity -6".
[*] [b]Alpha Genes' vicious UV sensitivity:[/b] no knockout. A dressed vicious-UV pawn stays awake in full daylight.
[*] [b]Alpha Genes' minor UV sensitivity:[/b] full sight instead of 90%.
[*] [b]Alpha Genes' lethal UV sensitivity:[/b] no sunlight burns. Real fire still burns, and burns already taken stay.
[/list]

[b]Sunlight bonuses are never removed.[/b] Alpha Genes' UV powered pawns keep their ×120% speed in the sun, dressed or not.

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Options > Mod Settings > Clothed Vampires. Every change applies immediately.
[list]
[*] [b]Slowness in sunlight[/b] (default on)
[*] [b]Bad mood in sunlight[/b] (default on)
[*] [b]Knockout and other sunlight conditions[/b] (default on, needs Vanilla Expanded Framework)
[*] [b]Burns from sunlight[/b] (default on, needs Vanilla Expanded Framework)
[*] [b]Which sunlight effects count:[/b] Genes only (default) blocks penalties from the pawn's own genes; Absolute blocks every sunlight penalty of the kinds switched on, whatever caused it.
[*] Debug logging (default off), and Reset to defaults.
[/list]
Without Vanilla Expanded Framework, the two switches that need it are grayed out with a note.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Alpha Genes[/b] and [b]Vanilla Expanded Framework[/b]: supported, see above.
[*] [b]UnderWhere[/b] and [b]Undergarments Core[/b]: their underwear is recognised and doesn't count as clothing.
[*] [b]Combat Extended[/b]: works alongside it (tested on RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7).
[*] [b]Big and Small[/b]: loads and runs alongside it.
[*] Your save files: the mod stores nothing in them besides its own settings.
[/list]
If you run into a conflict, let me know on the issue tracker linked above.

[h2]What it doesn't touch[/h2]
[list]
[*] Sunlight bonuses, in either mode
[*] Real fire, and injuries a pawn already has
[*] Night-time bonuses or plain daytime penalties from modded genes
[*] Where sunlight-sensitive pawns choose to wander or relax
[*] Pawns who aren't covered: they behave exactly as in the normal game
[/list]

[h2]How this mod was built[/h2]
Full transparency: the code for this mod was written by Claude (Anthropic's AI), as my development partner. It followed a structured process called [url=https://github.com/github/spec-kit]Spec Kit[/url]: a written spec of exactly what the mod should do, a technical plan checked against RimWorld's, Vanilla Expanded Framework's and Alpha Genes' own code, a step-by-step task list, and only then the code. Every decision along the way, from which effects count as penalties to how the settings work, was made by a person.

Every in-game test was played by a person in a real running game. On RimWorld 1.7, with a large mod list including Alpha Genes, Vanilla Expanded Framework, Big and Small, both underwear mods and Combat Extended, colonists with vicious, minor and lethal UV sensitivity were tested dressed, partly dressed, in underwear and re-dressed; real fire was tested on a protected pawn; every switch was turned off and on in a running game; Genes only and Absolute were compared; UV powered pawns were checked to keep their bonus; and settings were checked to survive a restart. The mod was also loaded without Vanilla Expanded Framework, and on RimWorld 1.6 a dressed vicious-UV colonist stayed awake in the sun. The specs, plans, test records and full history are public: [url=https://github.com/DaraynLB/ClothedVampires]https://github.com/DaraynLB/ClothedVampires[/url]
